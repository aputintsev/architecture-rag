Overview
The caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403, the pantheon of the drow, was "led" by dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, the Spider Queen, in the sense that she was the most powerful and widely worshiped drow deity. She dominated the drow race's lives and urged them into heavy infighting. She was opposed to varying degrees by the rest of the pantheon and did not share her power with other gods; in dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727-worshiping city states like 0158a791-a511-46cb-934e-d94ef787df5d the reign of her priesthood was absolute.
Vhaeraun, her son, was among the opposers. He had the largest following on the surface and the second largest overall among the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403. dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 considered him her real rival and enemy. The others were minor players.
In truth, the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403 were a pantheon only in a loose sense as most drow gods did not allow their followers to give any reverence to other deities; rather, they were simply a collection of gods, some related to each other and some not, whose followers were primarily drow, and thus they were bitter rivals as they clashed over the hearts and minds of the drow.
dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's domination over the majority of drow was to such an extent that even information on the other gods was not readily accessible to them. Vhaeraun's existence and agenda was only common knowledge among surface drow and in those parts of the 5191109f-1339-4b01-a9e0-a372a292027a where the Spider Queen's power was not absolute. Eilistraee, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's daughter and the only benevolent drow deity, was both on the surface and in the 5191109f-1339-4b01-a9e0-a372a292027a the subject of wildly inaccurate and wrong legends. Most of the rare few who knew of Kiaransalee believed her to be an undead madwoman who was a goddess only in her fantasies, while Selvetarm was practically forgotten. Only Ghaunadaur was capable of making his presence known or at least felt on some level by most drow.
Members
Between 1375 DR and 1379 DR, the drow pantheon downsized itself to a single deity, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727. Ghaunadaur left the Demonweb Pits, the others died. The dead members revived themselves over the course of the Second Sundering, in the 1480s DR.
The pantheon consisted of:
dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 (formerly Araushnee) : Goddess of assassins, chaos, darkness, drow, evil and spiders.
Eilistraee : Goddess of beauty, dance, song, freedom, moonlight, swordwork, and hunting.
Ghaunadaur : God of all things subterranean, jellies, oozes, outcasts, rebels, ropers and slimes.
Kiaransalee : Goddess of undead, necromancy, and vengeance.
Selvetarm : God of drow warriors and slaughter.
Vhaeraun : God of drow males, evil activity on the surface, territory, thievery, and arrogance.
Malyk : An aspect of Talos, who patronized wild magic, faded after Mystra could not endure him infringing on her monopoly over magic. He became a separate deity from Talos after the Second Sundering, god of chaos, rebellion, and wild magic.
Zinzerena : The original goddess of assassins and chaos. Her position was absorbed by dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 during the Time of Troubles. She returned, as a single being, after the Second Sundering, goddess of assassination, illusion, and lies.
Note that Keptolo was a well-known deity of the multiverse, but not a strict member of the Forgotten Realms drow pantheon. He was the god of ambitious male drow seducers, hedonism, beauty, and fertility; consort of dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727. It was unknown whether he was worshiped in a78cf514-59e2-45b7-8d35-ce316e032c93 or not.
History
The caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403 was rather young as a pantheon. This had to do with the fact that the drow came into being only after Corellon's Descent, when the dark elves were cursed and turned into them in −10,000 DR.
The Dawn Age
Main article: Dawn Age
Originally, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 (at the time known as Araushnee) and the twin deities born from her and Corellon Larethian—the elder, Vhaeraun, the younger Eilistraee—were dark elven gods and part of the Seldarine, residing in Arvandor.
Ghaunadaur was a very ancient deity, according to dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 far older than Ao, rumored to have emerged from the primordial ooze itself. In that age, it was worshiped by slimes and other crawling creatures, many of which had an alien intelligence.
Kiaransalee was a dark elven lich from the world of Threnody. After being banished by her husband, the king of Threnody, for her experiments on undeath, she managed to raise an army of undead and exact revenge upon the inhabitants of her former world, rendering it lifeless. She fled from the Seldarine's revenge into the Abyss, where she ascended to divinity as a demigodess.
War of the Seldarine
In −30,000 DR, Araushnee, with the help of her son Vhaeraun, gathered a host of evil deities opposed to the Seldarine, and convinced them to attack Arvandor, in an attempt to overthrow Corellon. Ghaunadaur entered the fray on his own without being asked by anyone.
She had planned for the assault to ultimately fail, as her actual goal was for his consort to be killed during the battle and to replace him as Coronal of Arvandor. She tricked Eilistraee into dangerously injuring her own father, but the Weaver's conspiracy was ultimately thwarted by Sehanine Moonbow, and Corellon's life was saved. After her betrayal was revealed, Araushnee was banished by Corellon and turned into a tanar'ri, while Vhaeraun was simply exiled. Even though she was cleared from all guilt, Eilistraee chose to share her mother's and brother's punishment, because she knew that the drow would need her light in the times to come.
The wizard Mordenkainen recorded a different version of this story. According to him, all elves, including the ones who would become the deities of the Seldarine and of the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403, emerged from the blood that Corellon lost in a fight against Gruumsh. Like their god, the first elves had shape-changing powers, and freedom with them. The Protector took some favorites, who were made deities: among them, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 was the most privileged. She saw that other races made something out of their lives, and made the argument that the elves should do the same by casting off their shape-changing powers in exchange of a fixed form that would allow them to gain dominion over everything. Every single elf agreed and chose such a form, but Corellon was revolted by their decision and railed against dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727. The god was stopped with the argument that nobody of his blood should be attacked, and, while he was thinking about it, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 used the opportunity to attempt to murder him. Realizing the extent of dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's betrayal, many elves intervened to stop her, but some remained loyal to the Spider Queen: the latter became the dark elves and their gods, and dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 became a demon lord of the Abyss. Eilistraee was an exception in this version too, for she did not side with dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, but still chose to follow the dark elves.
Aftermath
Araushnee took the name of dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, and made the 66th layer of the Abyss, the Demonweb Pits, her new home. She had to struggle to gain control over the layer, fighting the power of both Ghaunadaur and Kiaransalee, but neither of them were able to prevent dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 from reaching supremacy. Kiaransalee was subdued by the Spider Queen, reduced to an unwilling vassal. Ghaunadaur, on the other hand, became the author of his own defeat, as his failure to seduce and subsume dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 drove him to kill and steal the intelligence from his worshipers in a fit of rage, leading to a great loss of followers, and therefore of power.
Meanwhile, Eilistraee and Vhaeraun were active on a78cf514-59e2-45b7-8d35-ce316e032c93, home of, among others, the dark elves. With time, Vhaeraun gained a strong influence in southern 770f5340-cb05-49ab-a0e7-effb646db3b0, becoming a major patron of the elves of Ilythiir, and the driving force behind their expansion of territory and and power. Ghaunadaur's faith also set foot on Ilythiir and filled a similar but secondary role. Eilistraee strove against her brother's corruption of the Ilythiiri, but in the end it was to no avail, and she only gained small groups of dark elven followers in the region.
After her banishment, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's attention started to turn towards a78cf514-59e2-45b7-8d35-ce316e032c93 only when the moon elf Kethryllia Amarillis intruded into the Demonweb to retrieve her lover. Kiaransalee helped her find her direction and dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 did not interfere with the elf taking her lover back. By this point, dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 had already come up with the idea to regain her divinity and used her scrying magic on the elves to find out which world they came from, therefore learning about a78cf514-59e2-45b7-8d35-ce316e032c93. Her interest was immediately captured by the presence of dark elf followers of Vhaeraun, and she was delighted by the ambition and ruthlessness in the hearts of the Ilythiiri, deciding to spread her taint among them and to use them as the instruments of her vengeance.
The First Flowering
Main article: First Flowering
During this age the green and dark elven kingdom of Miyeritar was founded by political refugees from Aryvandaar. Eilistraee became a major patroness of the nation, which would later grow into one of the greatest centers of magic and art of Faerun.
dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 kept working to spread her influence among the dark elves of Ilythiir. Her activities poisoned the formerly peaceful relationships between Ilythiir and the newer elven realms. It led to the casting of the First Sundering to create a dark elf-free piece of land, in −17,600 DR. The collateral damage caused the destruction of a great part of Ilythiir (including its capital, Atorrnash), and the death of many followers of Vhaeraun with it. The Masked Lord's influence ebbed with the loss of his worshipers, and his savage efforts were undermined by the ongoing conflict between him and Eilistraee, granting dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 and Ghaunadaur the opportunity to fill the void.
The Crown Wars
Main article: Crown Wars
The Crown Wars had deep consequences on the deities that would later form the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403. For example, the drow race came into existence, fulfilling the core requirement for the existence of something like a drow pantheon.
First Crown War
The series of conflict started as an Aryvandaari military campaign to conquer Miyeritar, which was quickly occupied, even though many of its people continued resisting.
Second Crown War
The dark elves of Ilythiir decided to war against Aryvandaar's allies. Believing that Aryvandaar would soon invade Ilythiir, Geirildin Sethomiir (llythiir's coronal) summoned and made pacts with Wendonai, a balor in dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's service, for power to defeat his enemies. The demon became the Spider Queen's tool to corrupt the Ilythiiri. The nation's aristocracy followed their royalty's example and bought power from other fiendish patrons. By the Fourth Crown War, Ghaunadaur, Kiaransalee and Vhaeraun provided Ilythiir with similar power. The demonic counselors soon changed the nature of the war: the Ilythiiri nobles and warriors started to enjoy slaugther and violence, and their former purpose of avenging Miyearitar began to look less and less believable after centuries of massacres.
The Ilythiiri, who were still refining their worship of the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403, used to portray their gods as spider deities. Vhaeraun, for example, was depicted as a Masked Spider; Kiaransalee and dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727--or, arguably, Eilistraee--were on occasion confused with each other and portrayed as an undead spider with swords drawn. The drow gods were at times portrayed in poses that could lead to think to subordination: for example, in at least one depiction, small spiders representing each deity (some masked, some with skeletal features or melted faces, others singing praises) were all linked to a larger spider representing dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, in a manner resembling spiderlings that had just been hatched. However, all those were only the results of how some Ilythiiri interpreted the relationships among their deities, and the main gods of the nation (dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, Ghaunadaur, Vhaeraun and Kiaransalee) personally killed their priests for such depictions.
Dark Disaster
During the interim years between the third and fourth Crown War, the Dark Disaster struck Miyeritar in −10,500 DR. It was a magical cataclysm unleashed by Aryvandaar on their own territory, an accusation not based on evidence. Although many inhabitants had already left Miyeritar, the majority of those who stayed were killed by the cataclysm, many of the Dark Dancer's followers among them. In fact, Eilistraee lost most of her people during this event, which severely weakened the goddess' power. This downward trend continued until her church virtually collapsed when the transformation of the dark elves into drow kicked in, and with the rise of dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 and Ghaundaur.
Fourth Crown War
Ilythiir accused Aryvandaar for the Dark Disaster. As a retaliatory assault, they laid waste on Shantel Othreier, by this point Aryvandaar's territory. The remaining elven realms decided to curse all dark elves (including Eilistraee's followers) by channeling the power of Corellon Larethian. The dark elves were turned into drow and violently exiled into the 5191109f-1339-4b01-a9e0-a372a292027a -- an event known as "Descent".
Post Crown Wars (−9000 DR to 1372 DR)
dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 became the dominating deity of the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403. She turned drow society into an oppressive theocratic and matriarchal society, where males were less than second class citizens. Under the pretense to cull the weak and strengthen the overall power of the race, she forced the drow against each other in endless infighting. All towards the goal of hers and the drow's vengeance against Corellon. In truth, this was all a scam. dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 had so much fun at urging and seeing the drow fighting themselves, that she never put coherent efforts regarding the supposed goal, the aforementioned vengeance. To remain in power, she directed her clergy to mitigate and slow any kind of change and progress in drow society and worked towards extinguishing the drow's desire to return to the surface.
Vhaeraun strove to counter his mother's influence and tyranny over the drow. His goal was to shatter the order dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 was trying to impose on the drow, unite his people and then take back all that they had lost after the Crown Wars, including their place on the surface. He attracted especially male drow, due to his call for gender equality, those drow who wanted change and growth in their society, be it of societal, economic or territorial nature, and those drow living on the surface. While he could not challenge his mother directly, he acted in the shadows to disrupt the status quo. The Masked Lord gained the second largest followers base among the drow gods, and largest on the surface, resulting in dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 considering him her real enemy.
Eilistraee tried to be a mother goddess to her people and bring them the hope of a new life. She fought to lead them back to the lands of light too, and helped the drow rediscover the love, kindness and joy that were taken from them. She wanted them to flourish and prosper in harmony with other races, free from dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's tyranny and the strife that dominated their lives. Hers was an uphill battle, but despite the many hardships and setbacks, Eilistraee never gave up fighting for her people. Eilistraee's ideals were very different from the kind of mindset that the drow became used to under dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727, and few were the ones who heeded the call of her song. In fact, after the near collapse of her church, it was only in the centuries after Dalereckoning that her faith regained a degree of prominence in 770f5340-cb05-49ab-a0e7-effb646db3b0.
Ghaunadaur's drow worshipers consisted of those who grew tired of dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's tyranny and sought out alternatives. He never really cared enough for matters outside of his own ambitions, including his own followers, and thus his follower base, and with it his influence, never really grew. However, his presence managed to prosper in cities torn by strife, or where the presence of the other drow gods was weak.
During this period Kiaransalee managed to acquire some freedom from dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 by killing Orcus and taking the cold plane of Thanatos from him. She worked to erase all mentions and recording of his name in order to redirect his followers to her worship, but she still remained a little known power, even among the drow themselves.
The birth of Selvetarm
Selvetarm, son of Vhaeraun and Zandilar the Dancer (an elven goddess worshiped by the elves of the Yuirwood), became part of the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403 long after the Crown Wars. Seeing her people attacked by dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's minions, Zandilar sought out Vhaeraun and tried to seduce him, in order to gain his help against the Spider Queen. The Masked Lord however betrayed and imprisoned the elven goddess, who in turn would later manage to escape thanks to the help of Bast, a Mulhorandi demigoddess, but not before having conceived Selvetarm. Zandilar gave birth to Selvetarm shortly after having gained her freedom, and after having merged with Bast (forming Sharess).
Selvetarm walked alone for centuries, after having spurned both his parents, until he was found and befriended by his aunt, Eilistraee, to whom he grew very close. Selvetarm came to admire her goodness and appreciate her teachings, and the goddess hoped that, by teaching him her ways and redeeming him, he could become an exemplar that would aid her in healing the rift between the dark elves and the Seldarine. However, said hope and friendship ended in the late 3rd century after Dalereckoning, when dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 tricked Selvetarm into slaying Zanassu (a demon lord whom dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 considered her rival, as he claimed to have power over spiders), by promising him that doing so would gain him the appreciation of the Dark Maiden. Selvetarm killed the demon, but was overwhelmed by the demonic essence and he fell wholly to evil, ending up as dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727's champion. Spiteful dd665bc9-5adb-48aa-a6f0-236bce62b727 did this to prevent her daughter gaining an ally among the caa1d121-6119-4d31-acf1-e59491ecf403.
